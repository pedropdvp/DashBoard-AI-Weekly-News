How this is made. Every Monday at 08:00 Lisbon time, Claude reads official company blogs, major outlets (Reuters, Bloomberg, The Verge, TechCrunch, The Information, MIT Technology Review), Chinese tech press (SCMP, 36Kr, Caixin) and research feeds, then rewrites this page. Each analysis lists its sources.

Items marked (reported) come from press reports the company has not confirmed. Some figures reached us through daily news digests that summarize the primary report; those digests are listed as the source so you can trace them.
